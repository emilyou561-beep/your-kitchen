## Ramen Basics (11 components)

Authentic recipes link to these components: stocks, tare, aroma oils, chashu, ajitama, and homemade noodles.

- Clear Chicken Stock（`basic-chintan`）
- Tonkotsu Stock（`basic-tonkotsu`）

- Seafood Dashi（`basic-wafu-dashi`）

- Aroma Oils（`basic-aroma-oils`）
- Homemade Ramen Noodles（`basic-noodles`）

## Two routes

- **Quick:** supermarket chicken stock, tonkotsu concentrate, store-bought noodles and ready-made chashu; 15–60 minutes.
- **Authentic:** stock, tare, aroma oil and toppings made separately the way a ramen shop does it; 3–15 hours. The ingredient list is generated automatically for the chosen route.
